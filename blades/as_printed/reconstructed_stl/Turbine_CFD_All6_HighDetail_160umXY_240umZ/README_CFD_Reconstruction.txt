CFD TURBINE G-CODE RECONSTRUCTION — SIX SPECIMENS

Included: Baseline/Smooth, nominal Ra10, Ra20, Ra40, Ra80, Ra320
All dimensions are in millimeters. All six models are reconstructed from slicer G-code, not metrology scans.
The labeled Ra values are nominal specimen identifiers, NOT confirmed measured roughness values.
High detail: 0.16 mm XY, 0.24 mm Z; compact: 0.22 mm XY, 0.24 mm Z.
Different original slicing parameters exist: Ra320 used 0.12 mm layer spacing; the other five 0.10 mm.
Original slicer build positions differ. Align the geometries in CFD before comparing them.
The reconstruction is an approximation of programmed exterior toolpaths, not a measured print surface.
Validation note: binary STL file lengths and ZIP CRC validated here; individual five-variant exports had zero edge-pair mismatches/nonmanifold edges in prior checks. This does not replace CFD meshing validation.
